Twilio · 2022–2026

Redesigning Regional Navigation Across Twilio Console

When Twilio rebuilt its Console, the existing regional navigation broke. I identified the opportunity and led the full redesign — defining the system architecture, building a scalable component library, and coordinating implementation across every regional product in Twilio.

Role
Staff Product Designer — Lead

3
Phone Numbers, Messaging, and Voice regional experiences designed end to end.
The problem

To access regional products, users first had to navigate to the product catalogue and manually pin each product they wanted to use regionally. This flow was completely hidden — users were missing the regional setup entirely.

Once pinned, the same products appeared duplicated three times in the sidebar — once per region — with no explanation of which was active for compliance. The system was confusing, unscalable, and incompatible with the new Console architecture.

The solution

A single header-level regional switcher — visible only on regional products — replaced the double navigation entirely. A rules-based component library gave all product teams a consistent, error-proof system they could implement without designing from scratch.

I defined the system architecture, designed the component library, coordinated all regional product teams across Twilio, and directly designed the Phone Numbers, Messaging, and Voice experiences.
